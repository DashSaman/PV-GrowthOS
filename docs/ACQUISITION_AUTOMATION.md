# Existing-channel acquisition and sales followups

The ten original-tunnel shared accounts and separate 50 GiB lottery retain their existing schedule and budgets. No MTProxy or new free gift is introduced. Nothing is posted to the main channel.

`channel_acq_*` links attribute the visit and offer the most recent unexpired, receipted HTTPS-tested shared publication. The existing claim callback independently verifies current purchase history, both memberships, panel identity and real connectivity. Missing availability never creates a grant. `/share` and `/referral` expose voluntary sharing and the native purchase-bot referral entry; they do not send to a user's contacts or promise a GrowthOS reward.

Native invitation scheduling is 09:45 Tehran, with a 15-minute dispatch window and no historical catch-up. `AppConfig("pv_acquisition_policy")` must explicitly contain `enabled: true` and an integer `daily_cap` between 1 and 100. `PVG_PURCHASE_BOT_TOKEN` belongs only in the GrowthOS private environment, never database/source control. The bot must identify as the existing `pvnetwork_bot`. Two currently valid original-tunnel public posts, complete fresh SELECT-only history, an active ordinary main-bot user at home, and no previous invitation attempt are required. Failed, reserved and uncertain past invitations are excluded forever. One committed daily cohort reservation, permanent per-user reservation, at most two API calls/second and a 120-second dispatch bound prevent cohort replay. A missing send receipt stops the cohort without resending.

Production lifecycle rules use an aware UTC `not_before_utc` and integer `marketing_cooldown_hours`. Historical events are excluded before candidate limits; dispatch rechecks eligibility, trigger and cutoff. `trial_received_no_purchase` means a trial was delivered, not that the customer connected. Reservations are committed before network delivery. No trial-connectivity event is fabricated.

Mirza's existing referral commission remains its own responsibility. On October 8 the owner specifically approved changing only `affiliates.status_commission` from `offcommission` to `oncommission`. Existing 10 percent, referral-enabled and all-eligible-purchases settings were verified and retained. No duplicate GrowthOS reward policy, balance write or historical credit backfill is introduced.

`analytics.acquisition.source_observations` accepts explicit fixed start/end bounds and returns aggregates only. Payments preceding the touch, outside the window or duplicate invoice observations are excluded; native toman is used. First-seen/import observations and observed unpaid-to-active transitions are separated. Observation time is not bank settlement time and these aggregates do not establish incremental revenue. Existing historical events are not rewritten to invent a transition label.

Activation requires an immutable, reviewed CI artifact, healthy protected-service checks, current owned-publication proofs and bounded production templates. Instagram official access and accepted third-party placements remain external prerequisites. A enabled feature flag alone does not establish an operational acquisition channel.
